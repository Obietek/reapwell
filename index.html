Calories in. Calories out. Nothing else.

ReapWell reads the calories you burn from your Apple Watch and workouts, and the calories and macros your food tracker already logs to Apple Health, then shows the difference. One number, your daily deficit, on your iPhone, your Home Screen, and your watch face.

What it does

  • Calories out from Apple Watch active energy plus a resting baseline built from a few quick questions
  • Calories in and macros from whatever nutrition app you use, synced through Apple Health
  • A deficit or surplus goal you set yourself, with suggestions from gentle to aggressive
  • An activity level suggested from your last four weeks of workouts
  • Seven-day trends with a weekly estimate of weight change
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, plus an Apple Watch app and complication
No account. No ads. No subscription. ReapWell is a one-time purchase. It reads from Apple Health and never writes to it, and nothing leaves your devices.
